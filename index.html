How it works · One person, one stake

A different network. Owned by real people.

  1. 01

    Join as a real, unique you.
    Scan your ID once — the check runs entirely on your phone, and your data never leaves it. We confirm you’re a unique human; we never see your name, photo, or document. Under 5 minutes.

    What happens to my ID?

    The scan and the check happen entirely on your phone. Your phone reads your ID and generates a proof — a small piece of math saying “a real, unique human” — that carries none of your name, photo, or ID number.

    Only that proof reaches the network. The network verifies it to confirm you’re a unique participant — joining once — without your ID or document ever leaving your device.

    This is not KYC. There’s no file on you to leak, because we never hold one.

    Don’t take our word for it — the code is open source. Verify it yourself →

  2. 02

    Run a piece of the network.
    Set up the app once. A few times a day it wakes for under a minute, adds a block, and sleeps. Not mining — you won’t feel it. You become a Mobile Validator.

    How is this possible?

    Older blockchains make every machine redo all the heavy work. Here, the heavy cryptographic work is handled by a separate open market. Your phone just downloads the result — a small, verified snapshot of the network.

    To add a block, your phone:

    1. Wakes up in the background
    2. Downloads a few MB of data
    3. Does under 30 seconds of work to build the block
    4. Sends it
    5. Goes back to sleep

    A turn comes to your phone a few times a day, chosen at random and fairly. Between turns the app does nothing — that’s why it costs almost no battery, data, or speed.

  3. 03

    Earn stake from doing.
    Your phone keeps validating in the background. You vote, build, shape how you want.

  4. 04

    Own the network.
    Ownership is guaranteed by unique you and what you do.

Questions

Will it drain my battery?

A turn comes to your phone a few times a day and takes under a minute; between turns the app is asleep. Typical phones see minimal battery, data, and performance impact.

Is this mining?

No. Mining races specialized hardware 24/7. Your phone wakes briefly, builds one block, and goes back to sleep — the heavy work happens elsewhere.

How much data does it use?

A few MB per block, a few times a day.

Why do you need my ID?

So each person joins once. One real person, one stake — that’s what keeps the network owned by people, not bots or wallet farms.

Where does my ID data go?

Nowhere it can leak. The scan is processed on your phone; only an anonymous proof reaches the network.

Is this KYC?

No. KYC ties your name to an account. This proves you’re a unique human while keeping you anonymous.

Can I verify the privacy claim myself?

Yes — the code is open source. Verify it yourself →
